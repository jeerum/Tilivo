# Sales Ledger 2.0 (v0.12)

## Documents

- `SALES_INVOICE` normal invoices.
- `SALES_CREDIT_NOTE` full credit via existing inversion (partial credit is
  data-ready through payments/credits model).
- `ADVANCE_INVOICE` core model (document type + posting path readiness;
  allocation to final invoice documented as limitation).
- `RECURRING_INVOICE` generated drafts from templates.

## Numbering

Existing tenant-scoped invoice series/prefix with concurrency-safe atomic
allocation on issue remains authoritative; invoice numbers are never
generated by the frontend.

## Payments

- `payment_status`: UNPAID / PARTIALLY_PAID / PAID / OVERPAID.
- Manual payment records (`sales_invoice_payments`) update amount paid and
  status. They are explicitly non-bank records; v0.13 banking owns bank
  reconciliation/posting.

## Ledger / overdue

- `GET /api/v1/sales/ledger` returns invoices with amount paid, open balance,
  overdue days and AR summary.
- Overdue is derived from due date + open balance, not persisted.

## Reminders

- Reminder records per invoice with level, amount due, recipient, note,
  DRAFT status, history retained.
- Reminder sending is not automatic; e-mail integration remains future.

## Recurring templates

- Monthly / quarterly / yearly templates with lines, tax codes, language,
  payment terms.
- `POST /api/v1/sales/recurring-templates/generate` creates exactly one
  DRAFT invoice per period (unique `source_recurring_key`). No auto-issue.

## Output

- PDFs remain deterministic with invoice number, dates, VAT, references and
  customer snapshot; bank detail settings columns added for future rendering.
- Invoice `language` is per-invoice and frozen on issue.

## Accounting integration

Issue/credit still posts through Accounting Core + VAT engine; issued
invoices remain immutable; payment records do not post bank entries.

## Permissions

`sales.payment.record`, `sales.reminder.create`, `sales.recurring.manage`
granted to Owner/Admin/Accountant; existing sales permissions unchanged.
